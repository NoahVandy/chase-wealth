# Advisor bio — Chase Dalton

**Source document, supplied by the client. Received 2026-08-11.**

This is the bio Chase wrote and sent, reproduced verbatim. It is the **source of
truth** for his background, his beliefs, and his own phrasing.

Why it lives here: `about.html`'s narrative chapters and several lines on
`index.html` are edited derivatives of this text, and until this file existed the
original had not been saved anywhere — the site copy was the only surviving
record of it. That made it impossible to tell an intentional edit from a drift.

## How to use it

- **Quotes attributed to Chase must appear in this document.** Both pages carry
  first-person quotes in place of client testimonials, which the firm cannot
  publish without SEC Marketing Rule disclosures. A quote that is not traceable
  to this file is invented, and invented advisor quotes are worse than no quote.
- **Edit toward the site, not the file.** If page copy needs to change, change
  the page. Do not rewrite this document to match — it is a record of what the
  client sent, not a draft.
- **New copy should be edited down from this, not written fresh.** The voice on
  both pages is his, and it is his because it came from here.
- It is not a compliance-reviewed artifact. Anything lifted from it into page
  copy still goes through the advertising-rule review like any other marketing
  claim, and some of it does not survive that review unchanged — see below.

## Known tensions with published copy

- **"Disciplined habits ultimately lead to meaningful results in every area of
  life."** He means habits generally, but placed next to fee or investment copy
  it reads as an outcome implication, which is what the SEC advertising rules
  police. Deliberately not used on either page.
- **The scope boundary is not in this document.** The bio says the approach
  "integrates investment management, tax planning, estate planning, insurance
  analysis, and business exit strategies," and Chase later clarified (2026-08-11)
  that the firm does neither legal drafting nor tax return preparation. Both
  pages now state that division of labor; this file predates the clarification.

---

My name is Chase Dalton, and I am the owner and advisor of Chase Wealth
Management. I earned my undergraduate degree in Finance from Pittsburg State
University and a Master of Science in Finance from University of Missouri–Kansas
City thereafter.

As a fourth-generation business owner, I have always had a passion for helping
individuals and families navigate their financial lives with purpose and clarity.
Prior to founding Chase Wealth Management, I worked at a large local firm where I
had the opportunity to help hundreds of families with retirement, business, and
financial planning. I launched Chase Wealth Management with the vision of
providing more in-depth services to the families I serve.

At Chase Wealth Management, we are dedicated to helping clients steward their
resources wisely. We believe financial planning is about far more than
investments alone. Financial planning is about creating alignment across every
area of your life. Our approach integrates investment management, tax planning,
estate planning, insurance analysis, and business exit strategies into one
disciplined and intentional plan designed to help clients live with confidence
and purpose.

Our goal is to provide wise counsel through every season of life while helping
clients better understand how each area of their financial life is
interconnected. For me, success is defined by seeing clients become more
educated, more confident, and more intentional with the resources they have been
entrusted with.

Outside of the office, discipline and perseverance have always played a major
role in my life. I competed for four years on the track and field team at
Pittsburg State University, an experience that shaped my mindset toward
consistency, discipline, and long-term growth. I strongly believe that
disciplined habits ultimately lead to meaningful results in every area of life.

My passion for finance was deeply influenced by my upbringing. I grew up in a
family where my mother stayed home with us while my father ran the family
business in real estate. Like many families, the Great Recession brought very
challenging times. Although my parents worked tirelessly and remained steadfast
in their faith, I saw firsthand how valuable proactive financial planning can be
during uncertain times. That experience shaped my desire to help families prepare
for the unknown so that, regardless of what life brings, they know there is a
path forward.

Faith is central to who I am and to the work I do. I am a devoted follower of
Christ and strive to live in a way that brings glory and honor to Him. I believe
one of the greatest opportunities we have is to steward our resources in a way
that positively impacts both the Kingdom and the lives of those around us. The
intersection of faith and finance is where my passion lies.

When I am not in the office, I enjoy spending time with my amazing wife and our
families. I also have a passion for Northland Christian School and serving in
youth ministry at my church. In my free time, I enjoy faith, family, fitness, and
all things motorsports.
