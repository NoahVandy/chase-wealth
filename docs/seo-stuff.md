# SEO / AIO / GEO Strategy — Estate Planning Attorney

## TL;DR

- **Ranking on Google** and **getting recommended by AI engines** now share ~70% of the same foundation. That shared foundation is **E-E-A-T** (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness).
- Estate planning is a **YMYL topic** ("Your Money or Your Life"), so both Google and AI engines apply a **much higher trust bar** than they would to a low-stakes site.
- Three distinct goals, addressed separately below:
  1. **SEO** — rank for searches like "estate planner near me."
  2. **AIO / GEO** — be one of the sources AI engines cite/recommend.
  3. **Entity/reputation** — win the results when someone searches his *name*.
- **Compliance constraint:** a licensed attorney's marketing must follow state bar advertising rules (e.g., ABA Rule 7.1). Build attorney review into the content process.
- **Timeline:** this compounds over **months, not weeks**, especially in a high-trust vertical.

---

## 1. Shared Foundation — E-E-A-T (matters for both SEO and AI)

For a legal/financial topic, E-E-A-T is not optional garnish — it's the whole game. Google prioritizes it heavily for estate topics because of the fiduciary responsibility and long-term consequences involved.

**On-site checklist:**
- Verified attorney bios with credentials.
- Named author bylines on **every** article.
- First-person case studies.
- Primary sources cited for any legal claims.
- Visible awards and rankings.
- Clear privacy and terms pages.
- Client testimonials.
- **Schema markup (structured data)** tying it together so machines can parse who he is and what he does.

**Compliance (do not skip):**
- Marketing content must comply with the state bar's advertising rules — no misleading claims, careful handling of testimonials/results.
- Keep **attorney review in the loop** on AI-optimized content to stay on the right side of rules like **ABA Rule 7.1**.
- Build this review step in from the start.

## 2. SEO — The Important Levers

For "estate planner near me"-type searches, this is mostly **local SEO**, driven by three things Google weighs: **relevance** (query match), **proximity** (distance to searcher), and **prominence** (how established he looks).

**Practical checklist:**

| Lever | What to do |
|-------|-----------|
| **Google Business Profile** | Fully completed — correct categories, hours, photos, keyword-aware description. This surfaces him in the **map pack**, which sits above regular results. |
| **Reviews** | Volume, recency, and responding to them. A top trust signal for an estate planner. |
| **Directory citations** | Consistent **Name/Address/Phone (NAP)** across legal directories: Avvo, FindLaw, Justia, Yelp. Consistency matters as much as being listed. |
| **Content clusters** | Hub-and-spoke structure around his services (see below). |
| **Technical basics** | Fast, mobile-first, clean internal linking, `LegalService`/`Attorney`/`FAQ` schema. |
| **Backlinks** | Earned, never bought — local bar associations, community orgs, guest articles. Paid link schemes get penalized. |

**Content cluster structure:**
- A **hub page** per service, e.g. "Living Trust Attorney in [City]."
- **Spoke pages** answering questions people actually type: cost, wills vs. trusts, probate avoidance, blended families, etc.
- **Highest-value keywords** combine three elements:
  - **Location** — "near me" / city
  - **Service** — wills, trusts, probate
  - **Hiring intent** — "attorney," "free consultation"

## 3. AIO / GEO — What's Actually Different

AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude) **don't "rank" pages** — they synthesize an answer from a handful of sources and cite/recommend a few. To be one of those sources, three things matter more than in classic SEO:

### 3.1 Structure content to be *extracted*, not just read
- Put a **direct 1–2 sentence answer** right under each question-style heading, *before* the elaboration.
- Use FAQ sections and clean definitions.
- The AI wants a clean chunk it can lift.

### 3.2 Be specific and verifiable (the single biggest differentiator)
- AI engines favor **precise, verifiable claims** over general principles.
- ✅ "A revocable living trust in Missouri avoids probate on assets titled to it."
- ❌ "Trusts can help with probate."
- Vague, hedgy marketing copy is effectively **invisible** to these systems.

### 3.3 Build presence *off* his own site (most-missed piece)
- AI engines assemble a picture of trustworthiness from **across the whole web**, and lean unusually hard on **third-party and discussion sources**.
- **Reddit and YouTube** are among the most-cited sources in AI Overviews and ChatGPT responses.
- Get him mentioned, reviewed, quoted, and listed elsewhere: directories, local press, a podcast appearance, answering questions in relevant communities. This does more for AI recommendations than another page on his own domain.
- **Technical own-goal to avoid:** make sure `robots.txt` doesn't block AI crawlers (GPTBot, etc.).

## 4. Entity / Reputation — "When His Name Is Searched"

A distinct goal. When someone Googles his name or asks ChatGPT "is [name] a good estate planner," you're optimizing his **entity/reputation**, not a keyword.

**The play:**
- Make his **name/title/firm identical everywhere** (site, LinkedIn, every directory) so search and AI form one coherent picture — that consistency is what eventually earns a **Google knowledge panel**.
- **Own the first page** of results for his name: site, LinkedIn, directory profiles, reviews, press. No surprises.
- AI will only recommend a person it has effectively "read about" from **multiple independent sources** — so the earned mentions above double as the mechanism that teaches the engines he's real and reputable.

---

## 5. Measurement — A Reality Check

- **Track the old metrics:** rankings, map-pack position, review count.
- **Add the new one:** how often AI engines actually **name/cite him**.
- Traditional search volume is projected to drop meaningfully, and organic click-through falls sharply when an AI Overview is present — so "did they cite us" is becoming as important as "did they click."
- Tools now exist that query ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude/Gemini with your buyers' questions and show who gets cited. **Run a baseline before starting** so you can show the client movement.
